OpenBao outage: 76 s (502 for ~6 s, then 503 for ~66 s). Most of it is node
failure detection (54 s) plus PostgreSQL failover (7 s); OpenBao itself took
the lock 7 s after PostgreSQL had a new primary.
The Kubernetes Secret hello-from-openbao kept its value throughout.

Different from the expectation:
- "docker stop is like pulling the power": wrong. docker stop gives the node's
  processes a stop signal and 10 s, so OpenBao shut down cleanly and removed its
  active label; the openbao-active Service was then empty, which explains the
  503s (Traefik's "no pod to send to"; inferred, Traefik's answer body was not
  captured). A real power loss (or docker kill) was not tested.
- Outage 76 s instead of "about a minute".
- Flux controllers were replaced about 50 s before the ESO controller; I
  expected both after the same 300 s. Reason not checked.
